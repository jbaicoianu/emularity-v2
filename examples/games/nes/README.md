# NES games

Homebrew games the collection example (`examples/collection.html`) starts with. Each
except Kim & James can be redistributed under the license in the `.LICENSE.txt` file
beside it. The source for each is linked below. The `.png` files are title-screen
thumbnails, captured from the games.

| Game | Author | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thwaite (v0.04) | Damian Yerrick | GPL-3.0-or-later | https://github.com/pinobatch/thwaite-nes/tree/v0.04 |
| Concentration Room (v0.02a) | Damian Yerrick | GPL-3.0-or-later; verbatim copies of the ROM can be shared without source | https://github.com/pinobatch/croom-nes/tree/v0.02a |
| RHDE: Furniture Fight (v0.07) | Damian Yerrick | GNU All-Permissive | https://github.com/pinobatch/rhde-nes |
| robotfindskitten (v0.10) | Damian Yerrick | zlib | https://github.com/pinobatch/rfk-nes |
| Double Action Blaster Guys (v2) | NovaSquirrel | zlib | https://github.com/NovaSquirrel/DABG |
| Nova the Squirrel (v1.0.6a) | NovaSquirrel | Code GPL-3.0-or-later; art and music CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 | https://github.com/NovaSquirrel/NovaTheSquirrel/tree/v1.0.6a |
| Super Tilt Bro. | Sylvain Gadrat | WTFPL v2; bundled music and art CC-BY, credited below | https://github.com/sgadrat/super-tilt-bro |
| Kim & James | James Baicoianu | — | — |

Kim & James was made for Kim and James's 11th anniversary, in October 2026.

Super Tilt Bro. includes music by Kilirane, LemonyBounce, Ozzed, Tui, Tyson Tan, Zi Ye,
VGS Staff and Matt Hughson, and the character Pepper from *Pepper&Carrot* by David
Revoy (CC-BY 4.0). Its in-game credits menu lists them in full.

Nova the Squirrel's art and music are non-commercial (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Its author also
asks that the Nova character not be reused.
